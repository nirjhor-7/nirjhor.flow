Familiar quirky hours,
Restless faces around

Brewing madness,
Tea's been served

Gazing upon the sky,
Washed in dilemmas

Fluttering winds,
Shattered wings

Velvet agony
for promised Rain

Perplexed flings
for unpromised love

Cosmic synth in heart
with a Cup of tea in hand
