This time of the year is lovely, a bit mesmerizing too.
I am talking about the weather. 
It's the time when winter is almost there but not arrived yet.
The anticipation for a cold winter, after a long weird summer. 
It's all the poetic, romantic, terrific heart yearns for. 
With sweet rains in the afternoon, which gives you space to romanticize it with 'November Rain' of Guns n Roses. 
The slightly surprising light rain that melts within.
Yes, I can hear the winter winds whispering.
Whispering it's lullabies.
Beautiful sensory experience I had walking to my dorm the night before.
The time is ever strange.
Time is slipping by the fingers.
How long?
...
With only 42 days remaining of the year, all I wish myself is a lively life.
Do poetic justice to your soul, run for your life.
Live.
Breathe.
Write.
Un-become everything you are not.
Burn the bridge.
Set your life on fire.
Give yourself grace.
You got this.

I am writing this while enjoying Pomme's new album 'la forteresse'.
It's soothing. 

That's all for now.
Be present and less mechanical.
Be weird.

Pura vida.
*nirjhor*
