title: Dilemma and a cup of tea
date: 2026-10-08

Familiar quirky hours,
Restless faces around

Brewing madness,
Tea's been served

Gazing upon the sky,
Washed in dilemmas

Fluttering winds,
Shattered wings

Velvet agony
for promised Rain

Nameless feelings
for perplexed reveries

Cosmic synth in heart
with a Cup of tea in hand

Stranger in reverie
stares in the mirror

Fading in avalanche,
Into Hitherto unknown.
