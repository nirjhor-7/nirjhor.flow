Time goes away, <br>

round and round. <br>

Seconds of hope <br>

Or ever blazing dreams. <br>

The quite exhausted, <br>

Stubborn noise <br>

Almost tamed. <br>

The relentless pursuit <br>

of the most undisciplined, <br>

Scattered, obnoxiously unique <br>

your own peace of mind. <br>

Almost there, <br>

Almost there, & <br>

Almost there. <br>
